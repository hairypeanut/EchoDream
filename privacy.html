Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 16, 2026

Overview

EchoDream respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the app and how that information may be used.

Information You Provide

You may provide dream entries, fragments, notes, images, and mood selections when using the app.

Sleep and Health Data

If you connect Apple Health or Apple Watch sleep features, EchoDream may access sleep-related data you explicitly authorize such as sleep duration, heart rate, respiratory rate, and sleep stage data.

How Information Is Used

  • Store dream journal entries
  • Generate insights and summaries
  • Sync data across devices
  • Enable premium features
  • Improve app functionality

Cloud Storage

Data may be stored using secure cloud services such as Firebase authentication and database services to allow syncing and account access.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. EchoDream does not receive payment card information.

Data Sharing

EchoDream does not sell your personal data. Information may only be shared with service providers required to operate the app.

Security

Reasonable steps are taken to protect data, but no system is completely secure.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of EchoDream constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
